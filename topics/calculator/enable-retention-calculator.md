# Enable or disable Retention Calculator

By default, the Retention Calculator is enabled for your organization and appears in the admin dashboard under **Policies** > **Retention** > **Estimate Storage**.

Organizations with strict change-control processes can disable the Retention Calculator to prevent administrators from performing ad-hoc storage calculations against production retention figures.

When you disable the Retention Calculator, the calculator panel is hidden entirely from the **Policies** > **Retention** page.

## Enable or disable the calculator

1. Open the admin dashboard and navigate to **Org Settings** > **Features**.
2. Locate the **Enable Retention Calculator** toggle setting.
3. Switch the toggle to off to hide the Retention Calculator panel, or switch it to on to make the panel visible.
