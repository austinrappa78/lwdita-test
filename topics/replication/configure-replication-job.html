Configure a Replication Job

Follow these steps to configure automated data replication for your backup policies.

Prerequisites

Before configuring a replication job, verify that your network connection meets the target destination requirements.

Procedure

  1. Log in to the admin dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Policies > Replication.
  3. Click Create Replication Job.
  4. Select the source backup workload and choose a destination target site.
  5. Specify the synchronization schedule and bandwidth limit.
  6. Click Save to activate the replication job.

Note: Cloud targets require an active cloud storage subscription before activation.
